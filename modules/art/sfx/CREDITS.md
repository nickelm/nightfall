# Sound effects

All files in this folder are by Kenney (https://kenney.nl) and released under
CC0 1.0 (public domain). They were downloaded from https://www.soundcn.xyz,
which repackages Kenney's audio packs; only sounds marked CC0 there were used.
soundcn also lists Blizzard sounds marked "All Rights Reserved"; none of those are here.

The music in `../music/spooky-game-loop.mp3` is "Spooky Game Loop", supplied by the
team. It is the original track's bars 3 to 34, crossfaded at the seam and stored twice
in a row so that it loops without a gap (see `../music.js`).
